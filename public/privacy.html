Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what information Brightforge Labs LLC ("Brightforge Labs", "we", "us") collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers this website (brightforgelabs.app) and our apps. Some apps, like FadeReady, may also have their own privacy policy. If they do, that policy explains the details for that app.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run our website and apps.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • This website has no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics trackers.
  • You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time.

What we collect

On this website

This website does not ask you to sign up and does not use tracking cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may record basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This helps keep the site safe and working. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

In our apps

When you use one of our apps, we may collect:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Business details you enter, such as appointments, services, prices and records.
  • Payment details, which are handled by trusted payment companies (like Apple, Google or Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Device and usage details, such as app version, crash reports and how features are used, so we can fix problems.
  • Location, only if a feature needs it and you allow it.

How we use it

  • To run our apps and give you the features you ask for.
  • To keep accounts safe and stop fraud or misuse.
  • To answer your questions and send important messages about your account.
  • To fix bugs and make our apps better.
  • To follow the law.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our apps (like hosting, email and payment companies). They may use it only to help us.
  • With the business you choose to book with, when you use an app to book or join a line.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.
  • If our company is sold or merged, in which case this policy still protects your information.

How we protect it

We use strong security, such as encryption (HTTPS) and limited access, to protect your information. No system is perfect, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as long as the law requires. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, fix, download or delete your personal information. You can also ask us to stop sending you marketing emails. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have extra rights. To use any of these rights, email hello@brightforgelabs.app. We will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

Our website and apps are not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If a change is big, we will let you know in the app or by email.
