About us

Where bright ideas are forged into real products.

Brightforge Labs is the parent company that owns and builds our apps. We are small, focused and in it for the long run.

Our story

Why we started

People who run their own work are busy. Barbers, nail techs, shop owners and traders have no time to fight their tools. But the apps they use are often slow, confusing or ugly.

We thought they deserved better. So in 2026, Idael Fonseca Hidalgo started Brightforge Labs LLC, a software company in Florida, USA.

Our first app is FadeReady, for barbershops. Like all our apps, FadeReady is owned and run by Brightforge Labs LLC. Next come SizeGuard, a trading toolkit for traders and Nail Signature, an app that brings everything FadeReady does to nail salons.

What our name means

A forge is a hot fire where metal is shaped into strong tools. Brightforge is a place where bright ideas are shaped into real products. Labs means we never stop testing and improving.
